
A solo BDFL is a project where one person — you — is the "Benevolent Dictator For Life," the term open-source projects use (originating with Guido van Rossum and Python) for a founder who has final say on all decisions. "Solo" just emphasizes there's no team or committee yet — one person proposes, decides, and merges everything.

Writing a GOVERNANCE.md for that setup isn't about pretending there's a formal process — it's about telling future contributors (or future you) how decisions actually get made, who has authority, and what happens if the project grows past one person.
